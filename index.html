Meet the owner

Prameela

I learnt to sew sitting beside my elder sister, hemming handkerchiefs before I was allowed near the good fabric. Over thirty years later, I still remember the first blouse I stitched for a neighbour. It was a little loose at the arm, and she wore it anyway. I redid it free the next day, and that habit of getting it right has stayed.

Sri Durga Fashion Makers is a women's tailoring shop, and only that. I do not take men's work, so every hour in the shop goes to the way a woman's garment should sit, move and feel.

Prameela
